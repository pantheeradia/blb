# Bean Leaf Brew Keyword Ranking Snapshot

**Domain:** beanleafbrew.com  
**Sample date:** 1 November 2026  
**Search market:** Google India, English, desktop, `pws=0`  
**Scope:** 27 current SERP samples across brand, local, coffee beans, filter coffee, premixes, and product names

## Important methodology note

These are live public SERP samples, not Search Console or DataForSEO rankings. Google selected Rewari, Haryana as the browser location, so explicit Bengaluru/Whitefield terms were used for local-intent queries. Organic web results were counted; ads, Shopping, Maps, AI modules, and other SERP features were excluded.

Google returned between five and ten organic results per sampled page. Therefore, **“not found” means the domain did not appear in the visible first-page sample**, not that it ranks nowhere in the top 100. Rankings vary by location, device, time, language, and search history.

The installed DataForSEO skill was checked, but its paid MCP extension is not connected. Search volume, keyword difficulty, estimated traffic, and stable top-100 tracking are therefore not available in this snapshot.

## Executive summary

- Bean Leaf Brew ranks **#1 for its brand**.
- The site performs strongly for differentiated, exact product concepts such as fruit-infused coffee/tea premixes, usually at **#2**.
- Ambiguous proprietary product names rank between **#4 and #6** without the brand modifier.
- The site did **not** appear in the sampled first-page results for 15 broad, commercially important searches covering coffee beans, filter coffee, general premixes, and Bengaluru/Whitefield roasters.
- Amazon frequently outranks the brand's own site for exact Bean Leaf Brew product searches.
- The primary opportunity is not more branded optimization; it is building category and local landing pages capable of ranking for non-brand purchase intent.

## What the rankings mean

### 1. Brand demand is captured

The homepage correctly occupies position #1 for the brand. Instagram, Amazon, LinkedIn, IndiaMART, and Zomato also reinforce the entity on the branded SERP.

This is useful, but branded rankings mostly capture people who already know Bean Leaf Brew. The growth gap is non-brand discovery.

### 2. Differentiated premix products have genuine search traction

The site ranks #2 for four specific fruit-infused premix searches. That is the clearest organic strength and a defensible niche.

However, Amazon ranks #1 for each sampled exact premix search. The marketplace listings currently have stronger result prominence than the owned website.

**Opportunity:** Strengthen owned category/product pages with exact ingredients, sachet count, preparation, taste profile, nutrition, shelf life, shipping, reviews, Product schema, and internal links. Aim to move the website above the marketplace listing while retaining Amazon coverage.

### 3. Broad coffee intent is almost entirely uncaptured

The site did not appear in any sampled first-page result for:

- coffee beans online;
- freshly roasted coffee in Bengaluru;
- specialty coffee Bengaluru;
- roasted/ground coffee online;
- South Indian filter coffee powder;
- Whitefield/Bengaluru coffee roasters.

The current homepage, shop, and category pages do not provide enough local relevance, topical depth, trust information, or internal authority to compete with established roasters and specialist landing pages.

### 4. Broad premix demand is also uncaptured

The site has a distinctive fruit-infused niche but does not rank in the sampled first page for general coffee, green tea, black tea, or matcha premix searches.

This suggests the category architecture is too brand/product-led. Searchers need dedicated pages that explain product type, use cases, preparation, ingredients, sachet/pack formats, comparisons, and delivery.

### 5. Product names need brand and attribute reinforcement

“House Brew,” “Morning Buzz,” “Golden Crema,” and “Déjà Brew” are ambiguous names used by other cafes and products. The pages rank, but not at the top without a brand modifier.

Use titles and headings such as:

- `House Brew South Indian Filter Coffee (Coffee–Chicory) | Bean Leaf Brew`
- `Morning Buzz Medium-Roast Coffee Beans | Bean Leaf Brew`
- `Golden Crema Arabica–Robusta Coffee Beans | Bean Leaf Brew`
- `Déjà Brew Dark-Roast Coffee Blend | Bean Leaf Brew`

## Content plan for the next 60 days

### Commercial pages first

1. Rewrite and expand the whole-bean category.
2. Create a Bengaluru/Whitefield coffee-roaster landing page.
3. Rewrite roasted-ground/filter-coffee categories around grind, ratio, roast, and brewing intent.
4. Split general premix intent into coffee, green tea, black tea, and matcha landing pages.
5. Improve the four fruit-infused pages already ranking #2.

### Supporting guides

- How to choose coffee beans for French press, moka pot, espresso, and South Indian filter
- Whole bean vs ground coffee: which stays fresh longer?
- Coffee-to-chicory ratios: 100:0, 90:10, and 80:20 explained
- How to make South Indian filter coffee at home
- What is a coffee premix and how do you prepare it?
- Instant coffee premix ingredients, calories, storage, and shelf life
- Green tea vs black tea premix: caffeine, taste, and preparation
- Fruit-infused coffee: flavor pairing and serving ideas

Each guide should link to the relevant category and products; category pages should link back to the guides.
